Initially, I just investigated silicon by itself. You can see the poster [here](/assets/documents/comp_poster.pdf) and a cool visualisation [here](/demos/band-structure.html).

The full report is on the character of the band gap in the Si4Ge4 [001] superlattice, and can be found [here](/assets/documents/superlattice-report.pdf).
