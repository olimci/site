# Exhaust High-Temperature Sensor

The catayltic converter on my GTO died recently, and needed replacing, and unfortunately it took the exhaust high-temperature sensor with it.

The original part on the japanese-market GTO was the `MD172407`, which is discontinued and an absolute plain in the ass to get hold of, fortunately the `MD166032`[^1], which was used on the US 3000GT, and dodge stealth, seems to be electrically compatible[^2], so I fitted one of those and it seems to work[^3].

Just leaving this here in case anyone else runs into the same problem as me :evil:

[^1]: Seems to be also sold under part numbers `ETS20` and `SU6649`.

[^2]: The catalytic converter overheat light is often on for about a minute after starting the car, but doesn't seem to cause any problems.

[^3]: Required some minor modification and resuse of the original sensor.
